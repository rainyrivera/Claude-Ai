Can't fall asleep? Put your phone away and try this five-minute routine in bed tonight.
One: knees to chest. Hug your knees and rock gently from side to side. This relaxes your lower back after a long day. Thirty seconds.
Two: lying twist. Arms out, drop both knees to one side, then the other. Keep both shoulders on the bed. Thirty seconds each side.
Follow Rous Fit for routines that fit your real life.
Three: legs up the wall. Lie on your back and rest your legs on the wall. Just stay still and let your body relax. One minute.
Four: slow breathing. Breathe in for four seconds and out for six. Long, slow exhales help your body shift into rest mode. Ten breaths.
Gentle stretching and slow breathing before bed can help many people fall asleep more easily.
