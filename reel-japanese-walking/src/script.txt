Your daily walk might be wasting your time. Researchers in Japan found a simple fix, and it only takes thirty minutes.
It's called Japanese walking. Walk fast for three minutes. Fast enough that talking gets hard. Then walk slowly for three minutes. That's one round. Repeat it five times.
Here's what happens in your body. The fast part pushes your heart and lungs, and your thighs and glutes work harder with every step. The slow part lets your heart rate come back down, so you can keep going.
In their study, people who walked this way four or more days a week for five months got stronger legs, better fitness and lower blood pressure... while people who walked at a steady pace improved much less.
No gym. No equipment. Just a timer.
