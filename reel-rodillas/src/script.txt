If squats hurt your knees, don't quit leg day. Change the exercise.
One: box squat. Sit back onto a chair, tap it lightly, then stand up. The chair controls how deep you go, so your knees never go past what feels good. Twelve reps.
Two: glute bridge. Push through your heels and squeeze at the top. For many people with knee pain, stronger hips help, because your hips keep your knees in line. Fifteen reps.
Follow Rous Fit for more routines that respect your joints.
Three: step-ups. Use a low step. Push through your whole foot and keep your knee pointing over your toes, not caving in. Ten each leg.
Go slow, stay pain-free, and add a little more each week. If your pain is sharp or doesn't go away, see a professional.
