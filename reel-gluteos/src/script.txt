Squats alone won't build your glutes. Your glutes are three muscles, and squats mostly train just one.
One: hip thrust on the couch. Upper back on the edge, feet flat. Drive your hips up and squeeze for one second at the top. This is one of the best moves for your biggest glute muscle. Fifteen reps.
Two: reverse lunge. Step back and drop your back knee toward the floor. Lean your chest slightly forward to feel it more in your glutes. Ten each leg.
Follow Rous Fit, because next week we go even harder.
Three: side-lying leg raise. Lie on your side, toes pointing forward, and lift slowly. This trains the side of your glutes, the part that keeps your hips stable when you walk. Fifteen each side.
Three rounds, three times a week.
