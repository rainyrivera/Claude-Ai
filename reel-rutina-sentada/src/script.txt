If you sit all day, your body is paying for it. Here's a five-minute fix.
One: hip flexor stretch. Kneel on one knee, squeeze your back glute and gently push your hips forward. Sitting keeps these muscles short. Thirty seconds each side.
Two: glute bridge. Feet flat, push through your heels and lift until your body makes a straight line. Your glutes barely work while you sit. This puts them back to work. Fifteen reps.
Follow Rous Fit so you get a new routine every week.
Three: cat-cow. On all fours, round your back, then arch it slowly. This moves every part of your spine. Ten times.
Four: wall angels. Back and head against the wall, slide your arms up and down without arching. Ten reps to open your chest.
Five minutes. No equipment. Do it before work or on your lunch break.
