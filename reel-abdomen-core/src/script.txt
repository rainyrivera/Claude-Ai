A thousand crunches won't flatten your stomach. Here's what actually works.
First, the truth: you can't burn fat from one spot. But a strong core holds you tall and protects your back. Try this.
One: dead bug. Lie on your back, arms up, knees bent. Lower the opposite arm and leg slowly. The key: your lower back never leaves the floor. Ten each side.
Two: plank. Elbows under your shoulders, squeeze your glutes and pull your belly button in. Don't let your hips sag. Thirty seconds.
Follow Rous Fit so you don't lose this routine.
Three: side plank. Stack your feet and lift your hips high. This trains the sides of your waist. Twenty seconds each side.
Three rounds, three times a week. Add daily walks and good food, and that's when your stomach really changes.
